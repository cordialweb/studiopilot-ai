# 🎬 StudioPilot AI Vision

## What is StudioPilot AI?

StudioPilot AI is an **AI-powered Production Operating System** for the film and media industry.

It is not just a screenplay analyzer.

It is a collaborative platform that transforms production documents into structured production knowledge and coordinates every department involved in filmmaking.

---

# Vision

Our goal is to make StudioPilot AI the digital operating system of a movie production.

A producer uploads a screenplay once.

StudioPilot AI automatically understands the production requirements and assists every department throughout the entire production lifecycle.

Instead of simply answering questions, StudioPilot AI creates knowledge, assigns work, tracks progress, and helps teams collaborate.

---

# Production Knowledge Model

Every production begins with a screenplay.

The screenplay becomes structured production knowledge.

```
Screenplay

↓

Production Knowledge

↓

Departments

↓

Tasks

↓

Execution
```

The AI understands the screenplay and extracts:

- Projects
- Documents
- Scenes
- Characters
- Locations
- Props

These become permanent production assets instead of temporary AI responses.

# Long-Term Vision

StudioPilot AI should become the central operating platform for film production.

---

# Design Principles

## 1. Production First

Every feature must solve a real production problem.

---

## 2. Knowledge Before AI

Knowledge belongs in the database.

AI generates and maintains knowledge.

---

## 3. Multi-Agent Architecture

Each AI agent owns one business domain.

Agents collaborate instead of duplicating responsibilities.

---

## 4. Department-Centric Design

Every department receives its own tools, dashboards, and workflows.

---

## 5. Human in Control

AI assists.

Humans make final production decisions.
